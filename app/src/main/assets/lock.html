Detent is how strongly the two faces pull. Friction bleeds the spin off. At low detent and low friction a hard flick will spin it several times before it settles.

What this is, and what it costs

The case is three finishesreal geometry

Three lathes sharing endpoints, not one: a mirror-polished bezel, a satin band carrying a circumferential brush map, and a polished caseback rim, with a groove cut at the joint for the case seam. Metal reads as metal because of the transitions between finishes, which a single uniform surface can never show.

It is photographed, not floatedno assets

A studio drawn into a canvas — overhead strip, key, warm second key, floor bounce — through PMREMGenerator, plus a seamless backdrop and a real cast shadow. The shadow narrows as the watch turns edge-on, which is the cue that says the thing occupies space. No HDR file, about sixty lines.

The turn is physicsno tween

Potential U = −A·cos 2θ puts stable minima at both faces. Torque is −K·sin 2θ, friction is linear in ω. Pressing the crown applies an impulse, not a duration — the watch decides how long it takes.

It swings on its bowsecond spring

Angular acceleration feeds a damped spring on the bow pivot, so a hard flick makes the watch rock a few degrees and settle. That secondary motion is most of what reads as mass.

The dial is still Canvasports

Both faces are CanvasTextures drawn by the same 2D code as the Android build — hands, counters, guichet, engraving. The dial artwork is portable even though this shell is not.

This shell will not portbe aware

WebGL has no equivalent in a Canvas DreamService. Shipping this look means OpenGL ES shaders by hand, or Filament at roughly 4 MB — which ends the no-libraries rule the project has kept so far.
