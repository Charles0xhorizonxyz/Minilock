Atelier Complications

Everything a phone shows at a glance, arranged the way a watchmaker would arrange it. Battery is a power reserve, weather a thermometer counter, the next alarm a réveil marker, notifications a guichet. What can’t be read as an instrument goes on the card below the dial, set like a certificate. Turn anything off — the dial is designed to be complete without it.

Four cost nothing. Three need a permission this app has never asked for.

    The README promiseat risk

    Today the manifest declares no permissions at all, and the README sells that: “no network permission, accounts, advertisements, or runtime libraries.” Weather alone ends it. Everything else on this dial can ship without touching that sentence.

    Privacy on a screensaverby default

    A screensaver runs while the phone sits on a desk in a room with other people. Alerts default to senders, never bodies, and the guichet and card rows are suppressed entirely in ambient mode.
