What changed on the dial

ATELIER is gone. It was set in serif at 23pt with MINISCREEN as an 8pt sub-line beneath it — the collection name outranking the maker. MINISCREEN now takes the serif line itself, and EDITION 01 sits under it in the place the sub-line already held. One mark, no hierarchy to argue with.

The package is still com.miniscreen.atelier and the launcher label still reads Miniscreen · Atelier. Renaming the applicationId makes it a different app to Android — a fresh install, not an update. Say the word and I’ll do it now, while there’s nothing installed to lose.

How the pocket watch ports

Rotationnative

Android views rotate in 3D without a library. setRotationY(deg) with setCameraDistance(9000f) for a sane perspective, driven by an ObjectAnimator; swap the visible face at 90°, exactly as backface-visibility does here.

The case band14 + 10 slices

Real thickness, not a drawn edge. Fourteen discs stacked along Z for the case, ten more for the pendant and crown, so the whole object stays solid while it turns. Android: siblings at stepped translationZ.

The crown is the controlone gesture

On a real pocket watch the crown opens and winds it. Here pressing it turns the watch over, which means the settings need no button, no menu and no second screen. It is a focusable View, so it keeps keyboard and TalkBack access.

The card stays putdeliberate

Only the watch turns. The card below is screen furniture rather than part of the object, so it fades while you are setting up, and the case grows into the space it leaves behind.
